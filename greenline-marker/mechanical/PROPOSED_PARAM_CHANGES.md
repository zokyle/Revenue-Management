# GL-1 mechanical: proposed changes to design/master_params.yaml and DESIGN_SPEC.md (after rev C-EVT1 and its design review)

`master_params.yaml` was NOT edited. Part A records what happened to each proposal of the rev B list under
revision C. Part B lists the NEW changes the mechanical model asks for after rev C and after the design review of the
first rev C build, with numbers from `export/check_report.md`. Part C lists the decisions that are not YAML numbers.
Deviation IDs refer to `cad/deviations.py`. All of this is PROVISIONAL: nothing has been built.

Where the mechanical model already contains a proposed value, the column "In the model" says so; these values live in
`cad/params.py` (`MECH`) and are recorded as deviations until the YAML carries them.

## C. Decisions for the system owner (not YAML numbers)

- **One candidate cures three items: 0.2 mm more puck thickness (`envelope.thickness` 7.6 to 7.8), all of it above the
  LCD bonding plane.** It would raise the roof underside over the cameras from 6.8 to 7.0 (upper-field margin 0.054 to
  about 0.25 mm, B2), let the cover-glass recess be 0.2 mm deeper (glass 0.25 sub-flush, or 0.15 with a 1.1 mm crystal:
  B14 and B21 stop conflicting) without touching the 0.8 mm walls. Not modelled; it changes the envelope, the cradle
  lip and latch heights and the window height.
- **Step-proof claim.** By the review estimates the marker does not survive 800 N on the cover glass with the EVT crystal,
  and on turf the battery is pushed into the LCD above about 250 N. Either fund the test and FE programme (README
  section 9, test 6; B12, B13, B21) or record that the EVT units are not step-proof.
- **Base plate material** (B13): printed PA12 as now, or routed FR-4 / stainless. This also decides the thermal-bow
  question (README open issue 6) and whether the rim relief (B9) leaves the base carrying a hard-floor step.
- **Upper field of view** (B2): accept about +15 deg at the tolerance limit, or fund the 0.2 mm above, or move the
  spigots (B3, about 0.6 deg).
- **Grain opening** (B18): potted, coated or open.
- **Cradle latch** (README section 8, open issue 7): meets retention, strain and path requirements on paper with a
  peak strain of 2.05 % at full deflection against 2.5 %; a 3000-cycle test, a creep test and a pull test on first
  articles decide whether the necked 24 mm beam stays.
- **Adhesives**: perimeter seal (opaque black, thixotropic, sustained peel at 70 C on primed PA12), ball potting
  (dual-cure), hood gasket (black, soft, silicone-free), window adhesive (light-cure, low viscosity), LCD frame tape.
  None is selected.
- **Lens data for the OVM7692** (entrance-pupil position and diameter).
