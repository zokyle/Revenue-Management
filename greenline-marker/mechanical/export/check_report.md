Reason: visor face 18.4 to cube front 17.075 leaves 1.325 mm for recess 0.55 + wall 0.8 + clearance 0.25. Accepted in rev C (visor_window.min_wall_behind_recess = 0.525).
- **D05 tub** [recorded]: required: camera opening bevelled wider toward the inside. Implemented: no bevel: the opening is straight, +-2.0 wide and z 4.6..6.8, and as wide as the prism bay behind it (bay half-width 2.0); it runs from the floor of the window recess (y 17.85) straight into the bay, over a sill 0.525 mm thick. Reason: the arc ledge is 0.65 mm wide and the seal land 0.4 mm: any groove leaves a seal lip below 0.5 mm, and a chamfer on the 0.8 mm visor wall leaves less than 0.8 at its foot. Reason: there is no room for a key inside the seal line, and a key under the visor wall interrupts the seal land: the two lugs of the first rev C model sat in through-notches that connected the underside to the camera bays (design review; check 13 now proves the seal ring is closed).
