## Deviations from the specification

Status: accepted = accepted by the system owner (rev C or rev D); recorded = differs from the specification for the stated reason; open = needs a decision. Items marked YAML are detailed in PROPOSED_PARAM_CHANGES.md, part C (master_params.yaml is not edited by this package).

- **D04 tub** [accepted]: required: every printed wall >= 0.8 mm. Implemented: the wall behind each window recess in front of the camera cube is 0.525 mm: z 4.2..4.6 across the 4.0 mm bay (1.6 mm2 per camera); the bonded window backs it. Reason: visor face 18.4 to cube front 17.075 leaves 1.325 mm for recess 0.55 + wall 0.8 + clearance 0.25. Accepted in rev C (visor_window.min_wall_behind_recess = 0.525).
- **D06 tub** [recorded]: required: shallow glue channel if possible without going below the wall minimum. Implemented: no glue channel and no glue chamfer anywhere. Reason: the arc ledge is 0.65 mm wide and the seal land 0.4 mm: any groove leaves a seal lip below 0.5 mm, and a chamfer on the 0.8 mm visor wall leaves less than 0.8 at its foot. The 0.25 mm radial groove between base edge and side wall is the adhesive reservoir on the arc (it is filled down to the rim and modelled as part of the bead); the joint on the land is 0.40 mm high (FR-4 plate; 0.33 mm on the PA12 plate).
- **D09 tub** [recorded]: required: nose groove from the aperture edge to the visor. Implemented: groove 1.0 x 0.3 from the cover-glass recess edge (r 14.25) to the visor; roof thickened 0.3 mm underneath. Reason: the aperture edge is under the cover glass; the thickening keeps 0.8 mm under the groove.
- **D11 tub / lcd_glass** [accepted]: required: LCD glass assembled from below. Implemented: the glass is tilted in: rear edge first at 6 deg, shifted 0.55 mm toward the visor while its rear corners pass the seating ledge, then pushed back against the two rear glass ribs (which reach down to z 4.35) and hinged up (assembly step 9, path proven in check 14). Reason: rear glass corners are at r = 21.427 > ledge inner radius 21.35. Rev C accepted a flat insertion biased 0.25 mm forward (0.118 mm to the ledge, less than the print tolerance); the design review showed that the flat glass cannot go further forward because posts E stop it. Tilted, the front edge is still below the post feet while the rear corners pass, so the shift can be 0.55 mm (corners at r = 20.999).
- **D14 tub** [recorded]: required: top edge radius 0.6, bottom chamfer 0.3; cavity below z 1.2 = R 22 circle clipped at the visor face. Implemented: radius and chamfer on the arc only (visor edges sharp); below z = 1.2 the side wall ends on the radial plane through the outer D corner (+-13.8, 18.4), which leaves an open pocket about 2.0 mm wide under each end of the visor wall. The pockets lie OUTSIDE the seal ring (the bead runs round both D corners on the base land). Reason: a rounded visor edge would cut into the window recesses, which are open to the top face; the R 22 circle meets the visor plane at 33 deg and would leave feather-edged wall ends; the radial cut keeps the full 1.0 mm wall at the end face.
- **D18 cradle** [recorded]: required: four 3 mm feet pockets; PCB pocket underneath. Implemented: four pockets dia 8.4 x 1.0 for dia 8.0 x 3.0 mm adhesive bumpers, each with a blind core dia 5.0 behind it (the corners would otherwise be 13 mm solid); PCB held against the pocket ceiling by four M2 thread-forming screws (not modelled; printed as dia 1.7 x 0.5 spot marks, drilled dia 1.6 x 3 after printing because a blind 1.7 mm hole would trap powder); three lightening pockets underneath. Reason: '3 mm feet' read as 3 mm high bumpers (ambiguous); the pin force pushes the PCB out of its pocket so it needs positive retention.
- **D19 tub / main_pcb** [accepted]: required: 0.25 mm clearance on every drop-in fit. Implemented: the two PCB locating spigots (dia 1.0 x 0.6, tip chamfered 0.15) under posts B sit in a dia 1.15 hole and a 1.15 x 2.2 slot with 0.075 mm radial clearance, as the YAML specifies. Reason: a locating fit, not a drop-in fit: rev D keeps it and moves the pair to posts B (28.7 mm apart, the cameras between them), which cuts the fore-aft play at a camera to +-0.075 mm and the yaw to +-0.30 deg. 0.075 mm is below the +-0.2 mm print tolerance, so the spigots are a gauged feature (go gauge dia 1.10 at first article; dress an oversize spigot, never the PCB hole). They only locate until the adhesives have cured.
- **D33 ball feet / base_plate** [recorded] (YAML): required: plates accepted from 0.76 mm (PA12 0.83): the lower limit keeps 0.02 mm of epoxy between each ball and its land (YAML base_plate.thickness_accepted, N6); balls of grade 100 or better (E8). Implemented: true for a ball of exactly 1.0 mm. A grade 100 ball may be 0.0127 mm larger (basic diameter tolerance, ABMA figure as remembered): set to its protrusion on the gauge plate it stands that much higher, and 0.007 mm (PA12 0.014) of epoxy is left at the lower plate limit. Incoming rule of this package: the ball lot is measured and the lower plate limit is raised by the oversize of the lot (0.773 mm at the grade limit). Reason: E8 widened the ball grade from 25 (+-0.0025 mm) to 100 after the plate limits were fixed; no grade 25 ball in 316 stainless was found at 1 mm (docs/SOURCING.md). The protrusion, not the ball top, is what the gauge plate sets. Check 2 carries the row as this deviation.
- **D34 fixtures on the outer face (calibration seat plate, ball gauge plate, bridge gauge) / base_plate legend** [recorded] (YAML): required: turf-seat zero on a plate with three clearance pockets for the balls so that the base face seats (DESIGN_SPEC section 6, E11); legend at least 1.0 mm from the ball-foot holes (E10); balls set on a gauge plate (YAML base_plate.feet). Implemented: no fixture bears on printed legend. The base face seats on three annular pads (dia 7.0 round each dia 3.0 x 0.5 pocket, trimmed 0.75 inside the plate outline), not on the whole face; the ball gauge plate of assembly step 2 carries the base plate on three bosses dia 7.0 round its gauge holes, its face 0.3 lower elsewhere; every depth reading on the underside (ring crest, potting level, rim relief) is taken with a bridge gauge on three annular feet dia 6.0 / 1.6 round the balls. The legend is kept out of a dia 8.5 zone round each ball foot and 0.15 or more from each of these footprints (checks 15 and 18). Reason: a printed legend stands about 0.02 mm proud of the mask (0.03 at most) and the tub rim is only 0.25 above the base plane. On a full-face hard seat the marker would rest on ink islands, on a bowed plate or on the rim (0.02 mm across the foot triangle is about 0.8 mrad); on a flat gauge plate the ink would add itself to the ball protrusion (0.1 +-0.02) and take the same 0.03 from the epoxy between ball and land (0.02 at the lower plate limit); a depth gauge resting on legend 1.2 mm from the grain opening would read the potting level that much deep against a never-below limit of 0.05. Three points next to the feet on bare mask are one reference plane for all of these, and they measure what E11 is about: the plane of the base face against the plane of the balls. A laser-marked legend has no height and would not need this.
- **D35 retarder_film (optional) / cover_glass** [recorded] (YAML): required: quarter-wave film, about 0.1 mm, on the underside of the cover glass, axis 45 deg to the LCD polariser; 1.08 mm of air between display and glass, so nothing else moves (E9). Outside the dia 22.0 aperture it lies over the roof ledge in the 0.13 mm gap that the tape leaves: 0.03 mm nominal, 0.007 mm with the tape at its thinnest and the film 0.01 thicker. Reason: the air gap of 1.08 mm exists only inside the aperture. A film (with adhesive) thicker than 0.11 mm would carry the glass off its tape ring: it is then cut to dia 21.5 and hangs inside the aperture (its edge visible). The diameter is set by the centring chain (film on glass 0.25, glass in its recess 0.25, tape ring in the recess 0.25): a film that reached under the tape would lift the glass off its seal. The crystal and its recess are round, so the flat is what carries the film axis to the tub (edge mark on the crystal set to the nose groove, +-3 deg). Check 2 carries the rows.
